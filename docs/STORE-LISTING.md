## Full description
Strategic Football is a 1-on-1 tactical football battle for two players.

How it works:
- Each player secretly drags their 5 players to plan the next move.
- Every 10 seconds the plans are revealed and executed at once.
- Crossing runs collide, passes get intercepted, tackles win the ball.
- Hold the ball to pass to a teammate or shoot at goal.
- First to 2 goals wins.

Features:
- Real-time 1v1 multiplayer: create a room, share the 4-letter code, play.
- Strategic formations (2-1-2, 3-1-1, 2-2-1).
- 20 national & club flags to play as.
- Practice mode vs an AI opponent (works offline).
- Fast matches — about 2 minutes per game.
- No ads, no in-app purchases.
