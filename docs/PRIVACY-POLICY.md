# Privacy Policy — Strategic Football

_Last updated: September 2026_

Strategic Football ("the App") is a casual, two-player, turn-based football game. This page explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

## Data we collect

**No personal information is collected by us.** The App has no accounts, logins, names that leave the device, or tracking/advertising SDKs.

During an online match, the game server receives only:

- A room join request containing a short code you enter.
- Your in-game actions (stick positions, passes, shots) required to run the match for both players.
- A display name you type on the main screen (used only to label your team during that match; it is not stored).

These are transient — the game holds them in memory for the length of the match and discards them when the room closes or is abandoned.

## Local data

The App stores your chosen flag and formation locally on your device (device-only). This never leaves your phone.

## Internet connection

The App connects to its game server only to host/play online matches. The standalone "Play vs AI" practice mode runs entirely on your device and requires no connection.

## Third parties

- The game server is hosted by a cloud provider (e.g., Render) which may process standard server logs (IP addresses, request times) for operational and security purposes.
- Distribution is handled by the Google Play Store, subject to Google's privacy terms.

## Children's privacy

The App is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the contact email shown on your Google Play listing.
